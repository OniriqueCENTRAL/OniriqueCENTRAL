Overview

ONIRIQUE PUNCH is a multiband transient shaper. It separates a sound’s attack from its body, and sets each one on its own: more snap, less tail, or the opposite. The sound can be split into one, two or three bands, each with its own settings.

1. The bands

  • BANDS: 1, 2 or 3 bands. The crossover frequencies are adjustable (150 Hz and 2.5 kHz to start; with two bands, only the first is used).
  • Each band has its ON and SOLO, to hear it alone.
  • The display shows, for each band, its level and the gain applied over time.

2. ATTACK and SUSTAIN

In each band, two envelopes tell the attack (what rises fast) from the body (what holds), and the tail from the body.

  • ATTACK (−100 to +100 %): boosts or softens the attack.
  • SUSTAIN (−100 to +100 %): lengthens or shortens the body and tail.
  • MODE: SHARP, MEDIUM or SMOOTH, the detection speed.

Because detection follows changes rather than levels, the processing acts the same way whatever the sound’s level.

3. Image and output

  • PAN and WIDTH (−100 to +100): the image’s position and width.
  • MONO, PHASE (flips polarity), SWAP (swaps left and right), LINK (detection shared by both channels).
  • MIX: processed and original sound. IN and OUT: levels.
  • DELTA: hear only what PUNCH adds or removes.

Tips

A kick that hits harder: 3 bands, ATTACK +40 % in the high band (the click), SUSTAIN −20 % in the low band to tighten it.

Drums with too much room: negative SUSTAIN on all bands, MEDIUM mode.

A more present vocal: 1 band, ATTACK +20 %, MIX around 60 %.
