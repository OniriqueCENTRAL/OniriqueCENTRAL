Overview

ONIRIQUE SHAPER is a spectral transient processor: it only cuts or boosts the frequencies that make up the transients, and leaves everything else untouched. Each frequency is compared with its own recent average, in dB: detection doesn’t depend on the input level.

In practice: soften a snare’s crack without touching its body, or bring out a guitar’s attack, only in the frequencies where it lives.

1. Cut or boost

  • CUT / BOOST: cut or boost the transients. DEPTH: the amount (down to −30 dB in CUT, up to +15 dB in BOOST).
  • SENSITIVITY: higher, more transients are detected.
  • SHARPNESS: the width of each cut or boost, from 2 octaves to 1/30 octave.
  • DECAY: recovery time, from 4 ms to about 1.4 s. DECAY LF/HF: below 5, lows recover more slowly; above, highs.

2. Display and sensitivity

The incoming spectrum; in red what is cut (in purple what is boosted); the sensitivity curve and its five points: detection low cut, three bands (low shelf, parametric, high shelf or tilt, ±18 dB) and high cut.

  • A point is dragged; Ctrl while dragging (or the wheel) sets its width; Alt while dragging makes you hear it; double-click turns it off.
  • A click opens it in its panel: shape (or slope), FREQ, SENS, Q, LISTEN.

3. Stereo, output and advanced settings

  • STEREO: Left/Right or Mid/Side, LINK (shared detection at 100 %, two mono at 0 %) and BALANCE (how much each channel is processed).
  • OUTPUT: MIX and TRIM (±12 dB on the processed signal only).
  • RESOLUTION (Normal, High), OVERSAMPLE (1x, 2x, 4x), WINDOW (Short, Medium, Long), PHASE (Linear, Minimum).
  • DELTA: hear only what is removed or added.

Latency at 48 kHz: 1024 samples with the Short window, 2048 Medium, 4096 Long; oversampling makes it longer. It is reported to the DAW.

Tips

A snare that cracks too much: CUT, DEPTH 4, a sensitivity band around 4 kHz. The body stays, only the crack softens.

More attack on an acoustic guitar: BOOST, high SHARPNESS, short DECAY.
