Overview

ONIRIQUE VOCAL MINI is a complete vocal chain on a single page: cleaning, equalization, compression, de-esser, effects, delay and reverb. Each knob does one thing, and does it well. Fewer settings, more mixing.

1. LIVE and MIX

  • LIVE (default): zero latency, to record while listening.
  • MIX: maximum quality once recording is done. The core is oversampled, the de-esser, de-plosive and gate look ahead, the noise suppressor works frequency by frequency. About 27 ms of latency, compensated by the DAW.

2. CLEAN and COMP

  • PLOSIVE: removes P’s and B’s. NOISE: hiss and room. GATE (THRESH, RANGE, RELEASE): noise between phrases; it acts before the effects and never cuts a reverb tail.
  • COMP: one knob for a soft leveler (LA-2A style), then a peak compressor (1176 style), then a safety stage. LEVEL and PEAK meters, and AUTO GAIN.
  • MULTIBAND: the amount, and the BANDS panel to set each band (THRESHOLD, RATIO, ATTACK, RELEASE, KNEE, GAIN).

3. EQ and PARAMETRIC

  • LOW, MID, HI, AIR: bipolar, with curves designed for vocals. LOW CUT at 90 Hz.
  • PARAMETRIC opens the 8-band EQ full size: BELL, LOW SHELF, HIGH SHELF (±18 dB), LOW CUT and HIGH CUT (12 to 48 dB/oct), NOTCH, and a DYNAMIC mode per band.
  • The display shows the vocal coming in and going out, the parametric curve (red), the knobs’ curve (pink, with a handle to drag) and the total curve (white).
  • With the mouse: drag a point (frequency and gain), wheel for Q, double-click to toggle, double-click in empty space for a new band, hold a point to hear it alone.

4. FX, DELAY and REVERB

  • DE-ESS: finds the frequency on its own, French CH included.
  • DRIVE, PHONE, DOUBLER, CHORUS.
  • DELAY: send, FEED, FILTER, TIME (at tempo), PING-PONG.
  • REVERB: ROOM, PLATE 1, PLATE 2, HALL or SHIMMER, with MIX, DECAY, PRE-DELAY and CLEAN (keeps the vocal clear in the tail). The panel opens SIZE, DIFFUSION, MOD RATE, MOD DEPTH, LOW CUT, HIGH CUT, WIDTH, DUCKING and PULSE (at tempo).

Tips

Record in LIVE to hear yourself with no latency, then switch to MIX for the final render.

A modern rap vocal: COMP high, AIR up, DE-ESS, a bit of DOUBLER, a little PLATE 1.

The 21 factory presets are a good starting point: they don’t use the PARAMETRIC, which stays free for your touches.
