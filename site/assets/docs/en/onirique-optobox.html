Overview

ONIRIQUE OPTOBOX is an optical tube compressor in the spirit of the great Danish classic from the 80s, with its mixing unit underneath. The front is the machine’s, painted in the range’s red, and the VU is lit by a slightly blue LED. A switch goes from the original generation (vintage mk I) to the current one (modern mk II).

No latency. Mono or stereo input, stereo output, stereo sidechain input. 0 VU = −18 dBFS, like the machine’s calibration: the threshold reads from there.

1. The optical cell

The rectified level charges the attack and release capacitors; threshold and ratio apply after that, with a very soft knee: a level held at the threshold is lowered by exactly 1 dB.

The cell follows the light with a slight delay and recovers in two stages: most of it fast, the rest slowly. The harder and longer it has been lit, the more the slow recovery weighs: it has a memory. That’s what makes the fixed mode program-dependent.

2. Times

  • manual: ATTACK (0.5 to 300 ms) and RELEASE (0.05 to 10 s) on the knobs.
  • fixed: 1 ms and 50 ms, the cell does the rest.
  • fix./man.: two networks at once. A peak shorter than the attack is caught by the fast network and released quickly; a longer passage is held as in manual.

4. Tube, sidechain and parallel

  • Tube and transformers: 2nd harmonic from the tube, 3rd when pushed, low-end saturation in the iron when it’s loud, a slight low bump and softened top. Everything that distorts runs at twice the sample rate.
  • SIDECHAIN BUS SELECT: int. (the compressor listens to what it processes) or ext. (the DAW’s sidechain input).
  • SIDECHAIN LOW CUT: off, 80 Hz or 220 Hz, on the detector only.
  • PARALLEL COMP.: from the original sound to the compressed sound; the original goes through the same filters to stay in phase.
  • METER: the VU shows input, reduction (GR) or output.

Tips

A smoothly held vocal: mk I, fixed, RATIO 3:1, threshold for 3 to 5 dB of GR. The cell follows the phrase without ever pumping.

An even bass: mk II, fix./man., LOW CUT at 80 Hz so the lows don’t do all the work.

The 15 factory presets start from the settings recommended by the machine’s manual, with a threshold placed for a source at 0 VU.
