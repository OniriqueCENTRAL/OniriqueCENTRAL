Overview

ONIRIQUE VERB is an algorithmic reverb designed for vocals, on an insert and with zero latency. A reverb is a simulated room: the vocal is sent into a network of delay lines that bounce the sound between them, lose a little energy on each pass, and end up forming a dense tail that fades gently.

The delay-line network

The heart of VERB is a feedback delay network (FDN) of 16 lines. Each line delays the sound by a few tens of milliseconds (from 29.7 to 101.6 ms, multiplied by SIZE), then sends it back into the network. A Hadamard matrix mixes the 16 lines together on every pass: the sound spreads out, and isolated echoes become a continuous tail.

  • DECAY (0.2 to 20 s): the time the tail takes to drop 60 dB. VERB derives the gain of each pass from it, band by band (Jot’s method): depending on the space, lows fade sooner and highs at their own pace.
  • SIZE : the length of the lines. Moving SIZE glides the delays like a slowing tape: no clicks.
  • DIFFUSION : at the input, six short all-pass filters thicken the attack (the tail is dense from the first milliseconds, with no grain on consonants). An all-pass in each loop smears every pass further.
  • MOD RATE · MOD DEPTH : each line moves a little, by a sine wave and a slow random drift. The tail lives, with no obvious chorus effect.
  • INFINITE : the network becomes lossless and takes nothing more in. The current tail holds indefinitely, like a drone.
  • Stereo input: each side feeds half the network. A mono vocal gives exactly the same network on both sides.

The six spaces (MODE)

Each mode sets the network size, early reflections, low and high decay times, diffusion and modulation differently. Changing mode crossfades the level, reflections and octave: no jumps.

ModeSizeCharacter
ROOM× 0.45Small room. Strong early reflections, highs that fade fast: the vocal stays upfront.
PLATE I× 0.75Bright, dense plate, long highs. The classic on a vocal (default mode).
PLATE II× 0.95Darker, wider plate, more noticeable modulation.
HALL× 1.45Large hall, spaced early reflections, lows that hold.
SHIMMER× 1.25An octave up is fed back into the tail: it rises and sparkles.
ONIRIQUE× 1.70Huge, dark hall, deeply modulated, with a veil of octave: it undulates and dreams.

Early reflections (ROOM, HALL) are the first bounces off the walls: each side mostly on its own side, a little from the other, to place the vocal in space before the tail arrives.

EQ IN and EQ OUT

Two five-band equalizers: low cut, low (shelf), mid (bell), high (shelf), high cut. The bands are set with the mouse directly on the curve.

  • EQ IN acts on what goes into the reverb. Removing lows here stops the tail from rumbling; at zero, EQ IN touches nothing (a neutral band is skipped).
  • EQ OUT acts on the tail coming out. By default, low cut at 150 Hz and high cut at 9 kHz: a clean tail that leaves room for the vocal.
  • WIDTH (0 to 200 %): stereo width of the tail, set in mid / side.

MIX, DUCKING and CLEAN

MIX

VERB’s MIX isn’t a simple crossfade: the dry vocal stays whole up to 50 %, while the reverb comes up. Above 50 %, the reverb is full and it’s the dry vocal that fades. On an insert on a vocal track, you almost always stay under 50 %, without ever losing the vocal. The lock keeps your amount when you change presets.

DUCKING

DUCKING lowers the reverb when you sing and opens it back up between phrases: the vocal stays clear, and the tail fills the gaps. An envelope follower listens to the dry vocal (10 ms attack, 250 ms release); above −40 dBFS, the reverb goes down, by up to −24 dB with DUCKING at 100 %.

CLEAN

CLEAN does three things at once, more or less strongly depending on its setting: it removes sibilance before it enters the reverb (a reverberated “s” hisses for a long time), dips the low-mids of the tail (where it gets muddy), and clears the old tail when a new phrase starts.

Tempo, STUTTER and PULSE

  • BPM : locks the reverb length (from 1 beat to 8 bars) and the pre-delay (from 1/64 to 1/4, triplets and dotted included) to the DAW’s tempo, or to a tempo set by hand.
  • PULSE : the tail breathes in time (from 1/2 to dotted 1/8), locked to the bar when the DAW plays.

Tips

Vocal in front, reverb behind: PLATE I, MIX 15 to 25 %, PRE-DELAY 40 to 80 ms (or 1/32 in time), DUCKING 40 %, CLEAN 30 %.

Dreamy ambience: ONIRIQUE or SHIMMER, DECAY 6 to 10 s, MOD DEPTH 50 %, EQ OUT low cut around 300 Hz to keep the bottom of the mix clean.

End of a phrase left hanging: automate INFINITE on the last word, then turn it off at the start of the next phrase.

Rhythmic effect: BPM on DAW, STUTTER in CHOP or RAMP at 1/16, STUTTER MIX 60 %.
