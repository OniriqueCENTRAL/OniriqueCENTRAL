Overview

ONIRIQUE L2A is an optical compressor: it levels a vocal without you hearing it work. Two knobs are enough: PEAK REDUCTION for how much it compresses, GAIN to make up the level. The VU sits in the middle, between them.

Mono or stereo. The latency is the one of the chosen oversampling, reported to the DAW.

1. The optical cell

The sound lights up a cell that lowers the gain with a slight delay, then comes back slowly, in two stages: most of it fairly fast, the rest over one to several seconds. The longer it has been lit, the slower the return. That’s what gives this soft, musical levelling that never pumps.

2. COMPRESS and LIMIT

COMPRESS: gentle compression, the ratio rises softly. LIMIT: a stronger ratio that holds the peaks more. The character of the cell stays the same in both modes.

3. MIX and OVERSAMPLING

  • MIX: the dry sound blended with the compressed one, for parallel compression, perfectly in phase.
  • OVERSAMPLING: OFF, 2x, 4x or 8x. The new latency is reported to the DAW.
  • METER: the VU shows gain reduction (GAIN RED) or the output level (+10, +4).

Tips

A steady vocal: COMPRESS, PEAK REDUCTION for 3 to 6 dB of reduction on the phrases, GAIN back to the bypassed level.

An even bass: PEAK REDUCTION around 50, the cell smooths the notes without killing the attack.

After F76: ONIRIQUE F76 catches the peaks, L2A levels behind it. Each one works a little, the vocal stays natural.
