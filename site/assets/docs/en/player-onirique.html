What it’s for

PLAYER ONIRIQUE opens a video in a window of your DAW, locked to your playback: you start the project, the video starts; you stop, it stops; you move the cursor, the picture follows. Ideal for composing to a music video, a film clip or a vertical video.

  • Drag an .mp4 or .mov file onto the window, or open it with the button. The window takes the video’s format (16:9, 9:16, 4:3, square…) and keeps it when you resize it.
  • The controls only appear when the mouse hovers.
  • Your project’s sound passes through the plugin with no change at all.
  • The chosen video, SYNC, sound and window size are saved in the project.

Sync (SYNC)

The plugin reads your DAW’s playback position and sends it continuously to the video window: every 30 ms during playback, every 500 ms when stopped. The window decides how to catch up a gap.

Why a single jump, and not a jump at every gap? A heavy video (phone, internet) takes time to seek: chained jumps freeze the picture. PLAYER measures how long a jump takes on the video, and aims straight at the position the DAW will have reached when that jump ends.

Free player (SYNC off)

With SYNC off, PLAYER becomes a player independent of the DAW: play, pause, position bar and loop. Handy for reviewing a passage without touching your project.

Formats and DAWs

  • Decoding goes through the system’s web engine (WebView2 on Windows, WebKit on Mac). H.264 + AAC (mp4 / mov) works everywhere; HEVC and ProRes depend on the codecs installed on the machine.
  • FL Studio : put PLAYER on the Master. On a silent track, “Smart disable” stops the plugin and the video pauses: turn it off for this plugin if needed.
  • VST3 on Windows and Mac, Audio Unit on Mac. Free.
