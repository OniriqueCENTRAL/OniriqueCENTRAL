Overview

ONIRIQUE WEIGHT is a tube program equalizer in the spirit of the 50s classic (EQP-1A): the same front, the same controls in the same places, and its famous low-end trick. It adds two characters: a low end with weight, a top end that breathes.

The front is a photograph: every part is modelled in 3D, and a knob you turn keeps its reflections in place, like on the unit. Processing runs at twice the sample rate (4 samples of latency at 48 kHz, reported to the DAW).

1. LOW FREQUENCY: a low end with weight

  • BOOST lifts the lows by up to +13.5 dB around 20, 30, 60 or 100 Hz (CPS). The higher it goes, the more the tubes thicken the bass with their harmonics (mostly the second), which make it audible even on small speakers.
  • ATTEN removes lows, down to −17.5 dB, with a gentler slope centred higher.

The low-end trick: BOOST and ATTEN together. The sub rises, and the muddy low-mids just above it dip. At 60 Hz, BOOST at 10 and ATTEN at 10 give about +4.7 dB at 30 Hz and −4.8 dB around 200 Hz; at 5 and 3, +3 dB and −1.8 dB.

2. HIGH FREQUENCY: a top end that breathes

  • BOOST up to +18 dB at 3, 4, 5, 8, 10, 12 or 16 kHz (KCS): a bell (85 %) extended by a very wide shelf above it (45 %), like the air bands of mastering equalizers. The top opens and stays light instead of hardening.
  • BANDWIDTH: the bell width, from SHARP to BROAD. It tightens as you boost.
  • ATTEN: softens the top with a gentle shelf, down to −16 dB, at 5, 10 or 20 kHz (ATTEN SEL).

3. Levers and readouts

  • EQ: down, the curves are flat but the tube colour stays.
  • POWER (OFF / ON): turns the unit off, blue light included. The signal passes as it came in.
  • Hovering or turning a control shows its exact value in dB right next to it; a double-click resets it.

Tips

The low-end trick on a kick or an 808: CPS 60, BOOST at 5, ATTEN at 3. The sub gains weight and the low-mids clear up.

Air on a vocal: KCS 12 or 16, BANDWIDTH towards BROAD, a few notches of BOOST. If the vocal gets sibilant, a bit of HIGH ATTEN at 20 kHz softens it.
