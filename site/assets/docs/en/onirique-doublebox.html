Overview

ONIRIQUE DOUBLEBOX is a five-voice doubler: the voice itself in the centre, two doubles on each side. Each voice has its place, delay, detune and level; a shared pitch and formant go to whichever voices you choose, and a MANIPULATE column transforms the timbre. Mono or stereo input, stereo output, 28 ms latency reported to the DAW.

1. The stage

Five points: LL, L, C (the voice itself), R, RR. Left to right, the stereo position; bottom to top, the level (−36 to +6 dB).

  • drag a point: pan and level at once; wheel: detune, one cent per step (Shift: a tenth);
  • double-click: mutes the voice; right-click: resets it;
  • multiple selection (Ctrl, Shift, or a box): everything acts on the whole selection.

2. The voice panel

  • DELAY (0 to 100 ms), DETUNE (±50 cents), LEVEL, PAN.
  • PITCH / FORMANT: give it the column’s pitch and formant.
  • MANIPULATE: apply the right-hand column to it (a red diamond appears next to its name).

A voice that’s only detuned is read slightly faster or slower, like tape: nothing is chopped, breath and consonants come out as they went in.

3. Pitch, formant, drift and EQ

  • PITCH and FORMANT (±12 semitones each), independent: pitch moves the note while keeping the vowel, formant moves the vowel while keeping the note. APPLY TO picks the voices that get them.
  • DRIFT: the doubles drift in pitch (up to ±14 cents) and time (±3 ms), each at its own pace, like a second take. RATE: drift speed. The centre never drifts.
  • EQ: two shelves, low and high (±12 dB). ALL processes everything, DOUBLES only the doubles.

Tips

A natural double: L and R at ±12 cents, 15 to 25 ms of delay, DRIFT around 30 %. The centre stays the untouched voice.

Ambient voices: PITCH +12 on LL and RR only, low level, a bit of SMEAR.

Presets are sorted by type (DOUBLER, PITCH, FORMANT, MANIPULATE, ROBOT) in the browser.
