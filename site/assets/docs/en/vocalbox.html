Overview

ONIRIQUE VOCALBOX is a modular vocal chain: you build your chain with the modules your vocal needs (cleaning, autotune, equalization, compression, effects), in the order you want. Up to 16 slots, and up to three copies of the same module.

And if you don’t want to set everything by hand, AUTO MIX listens to your vocal and builds the chain for you.

1. Building the chain

  • The + opens the module list, sorted by family. A click adds the module at the end of the chain.
  • Drag a square to change the order (with a crossfade, no clicks). Each square has its power button and can be removed in one move.
  • Clicking a square opens its screen, using the full space.
  • CHAINS saves and recalls complete chains; COMPARE switches between two settings to judge.
  • SIMPLE MODE: each module shows only its two or three essential controls.

The compressors have their rack front, with VU meter and a live reduction strip. The 1176 comes in two versions: BLACK (clean and tight) and BLUE (more coloured, mids forward).

3. AUTO MIX

AUTO MIX listens to your take (and a reference if you give one), then builds the chain and its settings: equalization from the gap between your vocal and the target, compression from the dynamics, de-esser from the sibilance, space from the reference.

  • You can talk to it in words: “warmer”, “more upfront”, “a bit of reverb”…
  • Four macros refine the result: PRESENCE, WARMTH, HOLD (how firmly it compresses) and SPACE.
  • The vocal’s role (LEAD, BACKS, ADLIBS, DOUBLES) and its pan position adapt; several VOCALBOX instances in a project see each other.
  • ONR Neural Learning: VOCALBOX remembers your habits (what you tweak after an AUTO MIX) and suggests them next time. MY PROFILE shows what it has learned.

4. Quality, input and output

  • Oversampling OFF, 2x, 4x or 8x: the tone, dynamics and saturation modules run faster than the sample rate, for clean highs.
  • IN and OUT with their meters, and AUTO GAIN: the volume stays constant whatever the settings.
  • IN (at the top): the plugin in circuit; A / B, COPY / PASTE and presets in the top bar.

Tips

The fastest way: AUTO MIX on your take, then adjust with the macros. Keep what you like, VOCALBOX remembers your tweaks.

A classic rap vocal chain: NOISE GATE, TUNER, PARAMETRIC EQ, 1176, LA-2A, DE-ESSER, then a little DELAY and REVERB.

Cleaning works best at the start of the chain, before compression brings the noise up.
