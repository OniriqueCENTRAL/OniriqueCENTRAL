Overview

ONIRIQUE PITCHBOX is a note-by-note pitch editor for vocals. The plugin gets the whole take, analyses it note by note, and resynthesises it at playback while keeping the formants. A note you move in the editor is heard right away, without reanalysis.

The grey curve is what was sung, the ember curve what will come out. The ghost outline behind a note shows where it came from.

1. Giving the take to the plugin

With ARA (Studio One, Logic Pro, Cubase / Nuendo, REAPER, Cakewalk, Mixcraft): insert PITCHBOX as an ARA extension on the track, it gets the take on its own.

Without ARA (FL Studio, Ableton Live…), use TRANSFER mode, inserted as an effect on the vocal track:

  1. click TRANSFER (the plugin is armed);
  2. play the passage to correct;
  3. stop playback (or click STOP): the passage is analysed.

Faster: drop the vocal file onto the window (WAV, AIFF, FLAC, OGG, MP3), then play one second of the clip: the plugin finds where it sits. From then on, it plays its corrected copy every time the playhead crosses that passage. The take is saved in the project with the edits.

One take per instance. If the clip is moved later, redo the transfer.

2. Editing notes

  • Drag a note vertically: snap it to the scale. Alt: free placement.
  • Hold the click on a note: hear it held, corrected (transport stopped, TRANSFER mode).
  • Double-click: exclude the note from correction, for a deliberate bend.
  • MODULATION: on selected notes, how much sung movement stays inside the note (vibrato, attack, drift). 0 flattens, 1 leaves it as sung.
  • Multiple selection: Ctrl or Shift + click, or a box drawn in empty space.
  • UNDO / REDO / RESET: RESET clears manual edits and keeps the global settings.

Navigation: the wheel scrolls up and down, Shift + wheel moves through the take, Ctrl + wheel zooms in time, Alt + wheel in pitch.

3. Global settings

  • AMOUNT: correction strength on notes you haven’t touched by hand.
  • SPEED: the transition time at the start and end of a note. 0 gives the hard, audible snap; 40 to 80 ms sounds like a singer finding the note.
  • FORMANT: shifts the spectral envelope (the vowel) independently from pitch.
  • DETECT KEY: sets the scale to the key found in the take.
  • EXPORT MIDI: the notes as they’ll sound, in a .mid file.

Correction puts notes in place without crushing the vibrato: it stays as sung, unless you lower MODULATION.

Tips

A natural correction: DETECT KEY, AMOUNT around 70 %, SPEED 50 ms, then fix by hand the two or three notes that need it.

Keeping an expressive bend: double-click the note to exclude it from correction.
