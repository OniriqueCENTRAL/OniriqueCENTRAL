Overview

ONIRIQUE ECHOBOX is a creative delay that travels, with a single knob, from the purest digital to the most worn tape: on one side exact, crisp repeats, with a pitch shifter in the spirit of 80s studio digital delays; on the other, warm tape echo, single or multi-head. Mono or stereo input, stereo output, no latency.

1. ECHO

  • TIME: head 1’s time, from 20 ms to 2 s; with SYNC, a note value (1/32 to 1/1, dotted, triplets).
  • HEADS 1 2 3: the playback heads, at one, two and three times the time, combinable.
  • FEEDBACK: 0 to 110 %. Above 100, the echo runs away and sings on its own.
  • MIX: dry only (0) to echoes only (100), both full in the middle. DELAY OUT: the echoes’ level alone, −30 to +6 dB.
  • FREEZE: freezes the tape and loops it, without loss.

2. CHARACTER, from digital to tape

The knob reads in two halves:

  • 0 → 50 (digital): fully left, each repeat is an exact copy of the sound, no filter, no saturation. Towards the middle, a very gentle filter barely rounds the repeats. Time changes almost instantly.
  • 50 → 100 (tape): the record head saturates more and more, each repeat loses highs (down to 2.6 kHz) and lows, a light hiss sets in, time glides and bends the pitch as you turn it, and SPACE takes on a spring sound.

3. COLOUR and EQ

  • TONE: repeats getting darker, or brighter and thinner.
  • PITCH: each repeat goes up or down from −12 to +12 semitones, cascading.
  • WOBBLE: wow and flutter on the tape side, regular modulation on the digital side.
  • PING-PONG: repeats bounce from side to side.
  • SPACE: the reverb the echoes fall into, a spring on the tape side.
  • EQ IN / OUT: low cut, low, mid, high, high cut, with the live spectrum. Drag the points with the mouse, double-click to reset.

4. The display

  • On the left, the machine: a memory chip on the digital side, two reels turning at tape speed on the analogue side, the three heads lit when they read.
  • On the right, the repeats a sound will produce: one mark per echo, at its place in time and as tall as it comes back loud; left above the axis, right below. With PITCH, a staircase shows each echo’s pitch.

Tips

An old-school vocal echo: CHARACTER around 75, SYNC dotted 1/4, FEEDBACK 35 %, a touch of WOBBLE and SPACE.

A rising effect: PITCH +12, FEEDBACK 80 %: each repeat goes up an octave.

Echoes that don’t muddy the vocal: EQ IN with a low cut at 300 Hz and a high cut at 6 kHz.
