Overview

ONIRIQUE EQ is an 8-band parametric equalizer, the same as VOCALBOX’s (algorithm, analyzer, bands, listening), as a separate plugin. It has three ways of processing, chosen in the menu at the top, each with its own advantages.

1. The three processing modes

ModeLatencyWhat it does
Zero Latencynone“Analog-matched” IIR filters in double precision: curve shapes stay accurate right into the extreme highs.
Natural PhasenoneA minimum-phase FIR computed from the exact analogue response: analogue amplitude all the way up, the phase of an analogue equalizer.
Linear Phase341 ms at 48 kHzA zero-phase FIR: no phase shift at all. For mastering; the latency is compensated by the DAW.

Each change builds a new filter on a separate thread, applied with a 50 ms crossfade: no clicks when you turn a knob.

2. The bands

Eight bands, each with its own type:

  • Bell, Low shelf and High shelf, Notch (narrow cut).
  • Low cut and High cut: 12, 24, 36 or 48 dB per octave.
  • DYN: the band becomes dynamic, with its threshold. It only acts when the sound in its range goes over the threshold.

A band is set with the bubble above its point (on/off, frequency, Q / gain, slope, listen, remove) or with the floating panel under the analyzer (type, slope, FREQ / GAIN / Q, previous / next band, DYN and threshold).

Listen: holding a band’s point (or the headphones) plays just that band.

Tips

Removing a resonance: a narrow Bell band, boosted hard, swept in solo listen until you find the note that rings; then cut it.

Taming sibilance without dulling everything: a DYN band around 6 – 8 kHz with negative gain: it only acts on the S’s.

Mastering: Linear Phase avoids any phase shift between bands; keep Zero Latency while recording.
