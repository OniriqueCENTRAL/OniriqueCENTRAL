Overview

ONIRIQUE CHANNEL STRIP is a complete vocal console strip: noise gate, console channel (EQ and compressor), two de-essers, saturation, 1176, vari-mu tube compressor and parametric EQ, in one plugin. The processing order can be fully rearranged, without clicks, and every module has its own power button.

2. Order and bypass

  • Each module has an order badge: change it, and the real chain shows under the title.
  • An order change or a bypass is done with a crossfade: never a click.
  • A bypassed module isn’t computed at all, and a veil passes over its card.
  • LISTEN on each de-esser: hear only what it removes.

3. Presets and oversampling

9 factory presets: Default, Clean Vocal, Rap Vocal, Aggressive Vocal, Warm Vocal, Lead Vocal, Adlib, Dark Vocal, Airy Vocal. A preset first resets everything, then applies its settings: you always get the same sound back.

Saturation can be oversampled up to 8× to stay clean in the highs; latency is reported to the DAW.

Tips

Start from a preset (Rap Vocal, Lead Vocal…), then turn off the modules the vocal doesn’t need: every bypass is click-free.

1176 then VARI-MU: the 1176 catches the peaks, the vari-mu thickens and glues. Two moderate reductions beat one heavy one.
