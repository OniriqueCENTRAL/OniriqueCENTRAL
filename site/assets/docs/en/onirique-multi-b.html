Overview

ONIRIQUE MULTI-B is an optical multiband compressor: it splits the sound into four bands and compresses each one on its own, with a soft, creamy optical cell. Lows that spill over calm down without touching the highs, harsh presence is held without dulling the air, and a vocal stays even from the start to the end of the phrase.

Mono or stereo. Latency is that of the chosen oversampling, reported to the DAW (none when OFF).

1. The four bands

LOW, LO-MID, HI-MID and HIGH, split by three crossovers (200 Hz, 1.5 kHz and 6 kHz to start with) at 24 dB per octave. Without compression, the bands sum back flat: the tone doesn’t change.

Each band has its own THRESHOLD (above it, the band is compressed), its GAIN (its level after compression: make-up, or dynamic EQ) and its SOLO to hear it alone. The tabs at the top pick the band whose knobs you set.

The compression is built for vocals: detection on the body of the sound rather than the peaks, a very wide knee, a gentle ratio. The lows react a little slower (no distortion), the highs a little faster (sibilance held).

2. Setting it in the display

  • Drag a white handle to move a crossover.
  • Inside a band, move up or down to set its threshold (pink line). Shift: fine adjustment.
  • A click picks the band without changing anything; the value only moves once you drag.
  • Each band’s reduction drops like a curtain from the top, with its value in large type. The white bar shows the band’s level, the background the output spectrum.

3. ATTACK, RELEASE, OUTPUT

Shared by the four bands. ATTACK (1 to 200 ms): how fast compression grabs. RELEASE (20 ms to 2 s): how fast it lets go; like an optical cell, a phrase compressed for a long time releases more gently. OUTPUT: the output level.

4. MIX and OVERSAMPLING

  • MIX: the dry sound blended with the compressed one, for parallel compression. The dry sound is delayed exactly like the compressed one: no phase dip, at any setting.
  • OVERSAMPLING: OFF, 2x, 4x or 8x. The new latency is reported to the DAW.
  • RÉDUCTION (reduction): the strongest of the four bands, live.

Tips

An even vocal: start from Vocal Smooth, lower the thresholds until 3 to 5 dB of reduction per band on loud passages, then make up with OUTPUT.

Harsh presence: low threshold on HI-MID only (De-Harsh), the rest at 0 dB: the band only goes down when it jumps out.

Finding a crossover: solo the band and drag its handle until you only hear what you want to hold.
