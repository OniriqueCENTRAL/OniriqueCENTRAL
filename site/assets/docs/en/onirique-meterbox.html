Overview

ONIRIQUE METERBOX is the meter to put at the end of your master: loudness to the standards, seen over time and not just as numbers; the spectrum, held against the shape of a finished track; and phase, on a goniometer.

The sound isn’t touched: what comes out is what went in, bit for bit, no latency.

1. When it measures, and against what

  • SYNC: measurement follows the transport, starts from zero when playback starts and freezes when it stops. PAUSE freezes integrated, range, maximums and history. RESET clears everything.
  • Standard: sets target and ceiling at once.

TARGET (−36 to −6 LUFS) and TP MAX (−9 to 0 dBTP) can also be set by hand (Custom).

2. Loudness

  • Integrated, large: the loudness of everything played since the reset, and its distance to the target.
  • The bars: true peak per side, momentary (0.4 s), short-term (3 s) and integrated. The white lines are the ceiling and the target.
  • Range: the loudness range (LRA).
  • True Peak: the highest peak between samples (signal reconstructed at four times its rate). OVER when it crosses the ceiling.
  • PLR / PSR: peak minus loudness. The smaller they are, the more squashed the master.

3. History and Waveform

  • History: loudness over time (momentary in the background, short-term in red, integrated in white, target dotted), over 30 s, 2 min, 10 min or everything. On the right, the time spent at each level.
  • Waveform: the waveform of everything measured, in a column, with peaks in red and RMS in light, and below it peak, RMS and crest factor figures.

4. Spectrum

The third-octave spectrum, 20 Hz to 20 kHz: pink noise is a flat line.

  • Live (filled) and Average (white, over 3 s, 10 s or everything).
  • Target: the expected shape of a finished track. The average turns red where it leaves it. Targets: Modern, Urban (rap, trap, bass music), Rock, Acoustic and Reference.
  • Capture: play a reference track, let the average settle, then capture: it becomes the Reference target, saved with the project.

Below, the difference summarised in four zones: Low, Low-Mid, High-Mid, High.

5. Phase

  • Goniometer: what’s identical on both sides rises in the middle; what’s out of phase spreads horizontally.
  • Correlation: from −1 (the sides cancel in mono) to +1 (mono).
  • Balance: the louder side. Width: how much of the sound is on the sides.

Tips

Before sending to streaming: Streaming standard, play the whole track with SYNC, and check that Integrated is close to −14 and True Peak stays under −1 dBTP.

Comparing with a reference: play the track you’re aiming for, Average on All, Capture, then play your mix: the red zones show where it differs.
