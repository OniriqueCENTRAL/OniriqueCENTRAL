Overview

ONIRIQUE RESOBOX is a dynamic resonance suppressor. It spots in real time what sticks out of the spectrum of a voice, an instrument or a mix (sibilance, harshness, notes that ring out) and brings it back in line with what surrounds it, frequency by frequency, without touching the phase, only when it’s there.

1. How it acts

  • DEPTH: the amount. At 0, the plugin returns the input unchanged.
  • DETAIL: the scale at which the spectrum is read. Low (a third of an octave), it finds broad bumps and answers with wide cuts; high (1/24 octave), it finds isolated whistles and answers with notches.
  • TILT: tilts the whole sensitivity towards the highs (+) or the lows (−), around 1 kHz.
  • SOFT / HARD: soft rounds the onset; hard cuts sharply and twice as hard.
  • ATTACK / RELEASE: how fast a cut settles in and lets go.

2. Display and sensitivity

  • The grey body is the input spectrum, the light line the output. The red curtain hanging from the top is the current reduction.
  • The white curve is the sensitivity: above 0, the plugin works harder there; below, it spares.
  • At start: low cut, high cut and three bells. Double-click in empty space adds a bell (eight max); drag sets frequency and sensitivity, the wheel the Q, right-click resets.
  • The selection opens the island: on/off, shape (bell, low shelf, high shelf, plateau), FREQ, GAIN, Q, and the band’s place in the image: STEREO, LEFT, RIGHT, MID or SIDE.

3. MULTIBAND mode

The spectrum is split into four bands (low, low mid, high mid, high), split at 150 Hz, 1 kHz and 5 kHz to start. Drag the lines to move the boundaries, drag inside a band for its sensitivity (±12 dB). A band’s island gives its solo listen, its MAKEUP (0 to +12 dB) and its BAL.

Each mode keeps its settings while you’re in the other.

4. The result

  • MIX, OUT.
  • AUTO GAIN: gives back the level the cuts removed (never more than 12 dB).
  • STEREO / MID-SIDE / MONO, LINK (at 100 %, both channels get the deeper cut) and BAL (spares one side).
  • SIDECHAIN: detection runs on the side input.
  • DELTA: hear only what is removed.

Tips

A harsh vocal: DETAIL in the middle, DEPTH around 40 %, a sensitivity bell around 3 kHz. Listen in DELTA: you should hear only the harshness.

Sibilance: raise DETAIL, fast ATTACK, a sensitivity plateau above 5 kHz.

A mix: MID-SIDE, and put a bell on SIDE to calm the sides without touching the centre.
