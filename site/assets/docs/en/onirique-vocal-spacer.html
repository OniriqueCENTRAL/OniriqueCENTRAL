Overview

ONIRIQUE VOCAL SPACER adapts a finished instrumental to make room for the vocal. Put it on the beat, send the vocal to its sidechain input, and it carves into the instrumental exactly where the vocal needs space, only while it sings. No latency.

1. SPACE

The stereo image module, in Mid / Side:

  • WIDTH (0 – 200 %): stereo width, 100 % = neutral.
  • LOW FOCUS: brings the lows (below about 160 Hz) to the centre, in mono. The low end is more solid and leaves the centre clean.
  • SIDE DETAIL: brightness on the sides only (shelf up to +6 dB from 3 kHz).

2. VOCAL POCKET

A 20-band dynamic equalizer, from 100 Hz to 12 kHz, driven by the vocal. A band is only carved if the vocal is present there and the instrumental masks it, and only while the vocal plays.

  • SIDECHAIN / STATIC: without sidechain, STATIC carves a fixed pocket shaped like an average vocal spectrum (body around 330 Hz, presence around 2.5 kHz).
  • MID / STEREO: carve only the centre (default) or the whole image.
  • FAST / NORMAL / SMOOTH: pocket speed (opening 15 / 30 / 60 ms, hold between syllables 120 / 250 / 450 ms, closing 250 / 500 / 900 ms). It follows phrases, not syllables or drums: no pumping.
  • DEPTH (0 to −12 dB), SENSITIVITY, RANGE LO / HI (also with the mouse in the display).
  • LISTEN: hear only what the pocket removes.

The display shows the beat’s spectrum before and after, the vocal (in pink) and the exact pocket curve.

3. RESTORE

  • KICK FOCUS: boosts only the kick’s attacks (62 Hz and the click around 3.2 kHz), up to +7 dB.
  • MID ENERGY: parallel compression from 400 Hz to 3.5 kHz, bringing energy back to the mids. In the centre, it steps back while the pocket works.

4. Connecting the vocal

Insert the plugin on the instrumental track (or bus), then send the vocal track to its sidechain input:

  • FL Studio: route the vocal track to the plugin’s insert, then pick the input in “Processing”.
  • Ableton: the plugin’s “Sidechain” menu.
  • Reaper: channels 3 / 4.
  • Cubase / Studio One: the plugin’s sidechain button.

Without sidechain, set VOCAL POCKET to STATIC.

Tips

A starting point: SIDECHAIN, MID, NORMAL, DEPTH −6 dB. Listen in LISTEN to hear what goes, then set DEPTH by ear.

A busy beat: LOW FOCUS to clear the centre, a bit of KICK FOCUS so the kick stays up front.

AUTO GAIN ignores the pocket: the beat never jumps back up when the vocal comes in.
