Overview

ONIRIQUE GLUE is a stereo bus compressor in the spirit of the centre-section compressor of 80s G-series consoles: the same front, the same controls in the same places, and the same way of “gluing” a mix. Clean at normal levels, a little creamy when you push it.

The front is a photograph (every part modelled in 3D); the VU lights up red while the compressor is in circuit. Processing runs at twice the sample rate, latency is reported to the DAW.

1. Why it glues

  • One VCA for both sides, and a detector that listens to their sum, like on the console: the compression breathes with the whole mix, and the image doesn’t move.
  • AUTO release lets go fast after an isolated hit and slowly when compression lasts. That’s the setting that glues elements together.
  • The knee is softer at 2:1 and firmer at 10:1.

2. Times and ratio

  • ATTACK: 0.1 / 0.3 / 1 / 3 / 10 / 30 ms. Slower lets the transients’ snap through.
  • RELEASE: 0.1 / 0.3 / 0.6 / 1.2 s, or AUTO.
  • RATIO: 2, 4 or 10 to 1.
  • THRESHOLD: −15 to +15 dB, 0 being −14 dBFS.

3. Sidechain, colour and parallel

  • SC FILTER: a 12 dB per octave high-pass on what the compressor hears, from OFF to 500 Hz. The bass no longer pumps the mix.
  • HR (headroom): the output stage’s margin, from 4 to 24 dB (20 by default). High, it’s clean; lower, the peaks round off with blended 2nd and 3rd harmonics.
  • MIX: 0 to 100 % compressed sound, for parallel compression.
  • AUTO FADE: pressed, the sound fades to silence over RATE seconds (1 to 60); released, it comes back. The console’s end-of-song fade.
  • IN: off, the signal exactly as it came in, bit for bit.

Tips

Gluing a mix: RATIO 2, ATTACK 10 or 30 ms, RELEASE AUTO, 1 to 3 dB of reduction on the VU. The transients pass, the mix holds together.

Drums that hit: RATIO 4 or 10, ATTACK 3 ms, RELEASE 0.1 s, SC FILTER at 100 Hz, then MIX around 50 %.

A bit of cream: lower HR towards 10 dB on an already loud bus: the peaks round off without squashing.
