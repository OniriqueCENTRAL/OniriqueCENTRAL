Overview

ONIRIQUE VISION is a multiband saturation and stereo dimension processor. It splits the sound into four bands, FIRST, SECOND, THIRD and FOURTH, and gives each its own saturation, movement and place in space: depth, width, density and life, without losing anything in mono.

Subtle, it stays natural; pushed, it gets creative. On a vocal, drums, bass, a synth, a bus or the whole mix.

1. The animation window

  • FIRST, SECOND, THIRD, FOURTH: a click solos the band (several at once is fine).
  • The dots at the top move the three crossovers; the handles set each band’s level (-18 to +12 dB, mouse wheel too, double-click: 0 dB). VISION works like a four-band EQ.
  • The two traces, left on top and right below, show what CURRENT, DRIFT and LEAN are doing to each channel right now. Almost merged without CURRENT, they spread apart and crackle as you push it.
  • ON / OFF under each band: its processing on or off (its level stays). HYPER MODE: double the drive.

2. CURRENT, DRIFT and LEAN

  • CURRENT (0 to 200 %): the band’s response keeps evolving, differently on the left and on the right (random spectral drift, dual-mono). The sound gains movement, width and depth, with no chorus and no change of character. Above 100 %: livelier, more experimental. On a mono track, the movement stays, without the stereo.
  • DRIFT: the band’s centre spreads apart, up on the left and down on the right. More width and separation; the mono sum doesn’t move.
  • LEAN (OFF, L, R): the band gently favours one side without sounding panned. Two slightly different spaces, ideal for separating elements.

DRIFT and LEAN never change the mono sum: what they add on one side, they take from the other.

3. Saturation and color

  • TAPE: magnetic tape, progressive compression, rounded transients, softened highs.
  • TUBE: valve, even harmonics, warmth, gentle compression.
  • TRANS: transformer; the lows saturate first, attacks get through, the low end gets dense.
  • SATURATION of each band: the drive depth. The average level is made up: you judge the character, not the volume.
  • INFRA: low-end weight, present mids, darker overall, mud kept in check. TERRA: a balanced hype curve, lows and highs lifted, mud tightened. SUPRA: air and openness, without harshness. A second click turns the color off.

4. SURGICAL, filters and levels

  • BOXY (200 - 700 Hz) and HARSH (2.5 - 4.5 kHz): a frequency and a cut each (down to -12 dB), on four faders. PRE: before the saturation, POST: after.
  • HP and LP at the input (before the whole circuit) and the output (after); at the end of their travel: OFF.
  • Input and output GAIN. GAIN LINK: the output compensates the input, to drive harder at equal volume.
  • DRY / WET: the original sound blended with the processed sound.
  • LINEAR PHASE: the four bands become linear-phase (100 ms of latency, reported to the DAW).

Tips

A wider, livelier vocal: CURRENT between 40 and 90 % on THIRD and FOURTH, DRIFT on FOURTH, SUPRA.

Denser drums: TAPE, SATURATION 30 to 40 % on FIRST and SECOND, TERRA; raise the input with GAIN LINK to judge at equal volume.

Separating two guitars: LEAN L on one band, LEAN R on the next.
