Overview

ONIRIQUE RISER makes reverse reverb risers for the vocal, in one move. On the vocal track, it captures the vocal on its own when you start playback in the DAW; when you stop, the riser is ready. Grab the waveform and drag it into the DAW at the bar shown: it ends exactly where the vocal starts.

Free, no licence.

1. Capture

  • During playback, RISER constantly listens to the last 2 seconds. As soon as the sound goes above −45 dBFS, the take starts (those 2 seconds included), with its place on the DAW timeline, up to 32 s.
  • Stopping playback or a jump (loop, click on the ruler) ends the take. Playback without vocal doesn’t replace the previous take.
  • INPUT OPEN / CLOSED: closed, nothing is captured and the take stays as is. Handy so you don’t re-record your lead by accident.

2. The riser

  • REVERSE (the big button): the riser length, 1 beat, 2 beats, 1 bar, 2 bars or 4 bars, at the DAW tempo.
  • FX view: the finished riser, ready to drag into the DAW. The pre-fade curve sits on it: pulling its point down makes the riser start quieter and arrive later.
  • RAW view: the whole take. Two handles choose the part (the syllable) that creates the riser; it ends where that part begins.
  • LISTEN / LOOP: hear the riser (or the chosen part), with the DAW stopped.
  • IN SYNC: RISER plays the riser itself at the right moment during playback, no file to drag.

The dragged file is a 24-bit stereo WAV, named with the bar and beat where it goes.

3. Effects and EDIT

In one click: PHONE, CHORUS, TREMOLO, SWEEP, WIDE. Clicking an effect’s name opens its menu (MIX and DRIVE, DEPTH, TIMING, START or WIDTH).

EDIT opens the deeper settings:

  • SPACE: ROOM, PLATE, DARK, HALL or SHIMMER.
  • SOURCE: how much of the vocal is taken at the start of the phrase.
  • SIZE: the reverb size.
  • CURVE: the pre-fade shape.
  • GAIN: the riser level (normalised to −1 dBFS before).
  • S-CLEAN: removes sibilance before the reverb, for a riser without “sss”.

Tips

The fastest way: play your phrase once, stop, choose 1 bar, drag the riser into the DAW at the bar shown.

A bigger riser: SPACE HALL or SHIMMER, SIZE up, and WIDE.

If the riser sits on the same track as the vocal, set INPUT to CLOSED so it doesn’t get recaptured.
