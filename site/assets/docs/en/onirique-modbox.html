Overview

ONIRIQUE MODBOX is a delay-line modulation effect: flanger, BBD chorus, dimension. A single line, with one to eight voices, going from surgical digital to lo-fi analogue circuit, and from barely-there to a wall of sound. Mono or stereo input, stereo output, no latency.

The display shows at the top what the effect does to the spectrum right now, at the bottom each voice’s delay.

1. CIRCUIT: from digital to analogue

  • CIRCUIT: at 0, a perfect delay line (windowed-sinc reading), no noise, flat response. At 100, a BBD circuit. In between, a continuous blend.
  • DRIVE: soft saturation of what enters the line, feedback included.
  • DRIFT: clock drift specific to each voice, slow (wow) and fast (flutter).
  • NOISE: hiss.

DRIVE, DRIFT, NOISE and STAGES only count as CIRCUIT rises; at 0, they’re drawn dimmed.

2. DENSITY: from thin to fat

  • VOICES: 1 to 8 voices on the line, their LFOs spread over the cycle (2 voices in opposition, like a Dimension).
  • SPREAD: the gap between voice delays.
  • STAGES: BBD stages, 256 to 4096. Its low-pass filter closes as the delay gets longer, and earlier with fewer stages.
  • BASS KEEP: below this frequency, the sound bypasses the effect (Linkwitz-Riley, 24 dB/oct).

3. Modulation and intensity

  • RATE / DEPTH: sweep speed and amount. STEREO: right LFO ahead of the left, from 0° (mono) to 180°. RATE 2 / LFO 2: a second LFO. SHAPE: sine or triangle.
  • DELAY: the delay at the centre of the sweep, 0.3 to 50 ms.
  • FEEDBACK: from −100 % (phase inverted: hollow, liquid) to +100 % (metallic, sharp). CROSS: how much feedback switches sides.
  • MIX: from input only to effect only.

4. TEXTURE and output

  • CRUSH: 16 down to 3 bits. DOWNSAMPLE: 40 kHz down to 300 Hz, with its aliasing. DISTORTION: hard saturation, up to 36 dB. Applied to the voices, outside the feedback loop: the direct sound stays clean.
  • AUTO GAIN: brings the output back to the input level (up to ±12 dB). OUT: final level.

A preset’s type is read from its settings: Vibrato if MIX ≥ 95 % and low feedback, otherwise Flanger under 8 ms of delay and Chorus above, plus Bit Crusher and Distortion.

Tips

A vintage synth chorus: CIRCUIT 100, VOICES 2, STEREO 180°, DELAY around 7 ms, slow RATE.

A flanger that takes off: DELAY 1 to 3 ms, FEEDBACK +70 %, high DEPTH, very slow RATE.

On a bass or a kick, raise BASS KEEP: the lows stay tight and centred.
