Overview

ONIRIQUE MASSIF is a stereo passive tube equalizer in the spirit of the big mastering passive EQ in a rack front: two channels of four bands, filters and an output gain, painted red. The front is a photograph: every part is modelled in 3D, and every position of every control has its own picture.

Processing runs at twice the sample rate, for faithful highs right up to the top of the spectrum (a few samples of latency, compensated by the DAW).

1. Lows and low-mids (bands 1 and 2)

The original passive network, as is:

  • bells going from very wide to narrow, that tighten as you boost;
  • cuts slightly narrower than the boosts;
  • shelves that resonate when you close the bandwidth: a bump before the frequency, a dip right after;
  • bands that load each other: two bands pushed side by side give less than their sum, and the low end stays round even when you insist.

Behind them, the tubes, and the transformer iron that thickens the bass when it gets loud.

2. Highs and high-mids (bands 3 and 4)

A hybrid. Each band is half that same passive network, half a discrete class A band: its width stays steady whatever the gain, and its shelf never resonates. The top opens with the softness of passive and the precision of discrete, without getting harsh.

The highs don’t go through the tubes, only through a very clean discrete stage.

3. One band, control by control

  • GAIN: 0 to 20 dB, up or down depending on the lever.
  • BOOST / OUT / CUT lever: in the window, BOOST lights up green and CUT red, like on the unit. Clicking the lit word puts the band back to OUT.
  • BANDWIDTH: from very wide to narrow; on a shelf, it sets the resonance.
  • FREQUENCY: eleven positions per band.
  • SHELF / BELL: shelf (low for bands 1 and 2, high for 3 and 4) or bell.

4. Per channel, and in the middle

  • IN: the blue light. Off, bands and filters are flat, but the tubes stay.
  • HIGH PASS: OFF, 22, 39, 68, 120 or 220 Hz, 18 dB per octave.
  • LOW PASS: OFF, 18, 12, 9, 7.5 or 6 kHz, 24 dB per octave.
  • Output GAIN: −6 to +4 dB.
  • LINK (in the middle): turning a control turns its twin on the other side; when engaged, the right side takes the left side’s settings.
  • POWER: off, the signal as it came in.

Hovering or turning a control shows its value right next to it; a double-click resets it.

Tips

Weight without mud: band 1 on BOOST, as a shelf around 60 Hz, and band 2 on CUT, fairly wide, around 250 Hz. The bands load each other: the low end stays round.

Air on a mix: band 4 as a shelf around 16 or 20 kHz, a few notches of BOOST. It doesn’t go through the tubes and stays clean.

Different stereo sides: turn LINK off to process left and right separately, for example on a stereo drum recording.
